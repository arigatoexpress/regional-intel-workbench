# Elite Net Solutions — Cold Call Script
**Universal phone script for permit-based outreach | 30 seconds**

---

## The Script

> "Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] from Elite Net Solutions — we're a veteran-owned network company here in Houston.
>
> I saw your [PERMIT TYPE] over near [NEIGHBORHOOD] just went through — congratulations on that.
>
> We specialize in enterprise WiFi and security systems for new residential builds — same hardware as commercial buildings, Ubiquiti and Reolink, no monthly subscriptions for the homeowner.
>
> We work directly with builders before construction starts, which is the easiest and cheapest time to do this right.
>
> Do you have 10 minutes this week to hear what that looks like?"

---

## If They Say "I'm Busy" / "Send me an email"

> "Absolutely, no problem. What's the best email? I'll send over a sample package for a similar-sized project — takes 30 seconds to look at and you'll have pricing to reference."

---

## If They Say "We already have someone"

> "Totally understood — do you mind me asking who you're using? [Pause.] Got it. Well, if that ever changes or you have a project where you want a second opinion on the spec, keep us in mind. Veteran-owned, we're not going anywhere. Thanks for your time."

---

## If They Say "Tell me more"

> "Sure — we use Ubiquiti UniFi access points, the same hardware you'd find in a 4-star hotel or office building. For security, Reolink NVR with local recording — the homeowner doesn't pay a monthly fee, ever. We've done projects in [Heights / Woodlands / Katy — use nearest match]. Typical install for a new build is [2-3 days]. Want me to put together a quick quote based on your square footage?"

---

## Key Talking Points (drop in as needed)

| Objection / Question | Response |
|---------------------|----------|
| "What does it cost?" | "For a 3,500 sqft build, typically $4,500–$8,000 installed. We can give you exact numbers once we see the floor plan." |
| "Consumer WiFi is fine" | "It's fine for renters. Your $700K buyer is going to have 40+ devices day one — a mesh router will cap out. We architect for that." |
| "I don't handle that" | "Who's the right person — the builder, architect, or does your buyer handle it?" |
| "Security cameras — we use Ring" | "Ring requires a $100/year subscription and stores footage in Amazon's cloud. Our system stores locally — no subscription, no third-party access. Buyers love that." |

---

## Call Cadence

1. **Day 1:** Cold call. If no answer, leave 15-second voicemail + send the SMS template.
2. **Day 4:** Follow-up email (use email template).
3. **Day 10:** Final follow-up call. "Just checking in — wanted to make sure my email didn't get lost."
4. **30 days:** Add to monthly newsletter list. Don't call again until a new permit triggers.
